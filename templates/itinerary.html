TRAVEL ITINERARY

Day 1: Arrival & Light Culture

• 11:00 → Departure from Manila
• 13:15 → Arrival at Noi Bai Airport
• 13:15–14:00 → Van transfer to hotel & check-in
• 15:00–16:30 → Hoan Kiem Lake & Ngoc Son Temple
• 17:00–18:00 → Hanoi Train Street
• 18:30–19:30 → Dinner local cuisine
• 20:00 onwards → Old Quarter walk

Day 2: Natural Attraction

• 07:00–11:00 → Transfer Hanoi → Ha Long Bay → Scenic drive to the UNESCO World Heritage site.
• 11:30–17:30 → Boat cruise, limestone karsts, island hopping – Sail through emerald waters and towering karsts.
• 18:00–20:00 → Dinner onboard or in Ha Long City – Enjoy fresh seafood with a view.
• 20:00 onwards → Leisure – Relax under the stars or explore the bay at night.

Day 3: Heritage & Nature

• 07:00–09:00 → Transfer Hanoi → Ninh Binh - Journey to Vietnam’s “Halong Bay on land.”
• 09:30–11:30 → Boat ride at Trang An Landscape Complex – Glide through caves and karst scenery.
• 12:00–13:00 → Lunch with local specialties – Sample Ninh Binh’s famous goat meat dishes.
• 13:30–15:30 → Explore Hoa Lu Ancient Capital – Visit temples of Vietnam’s first capital.
• 16:00–18:00 → Visit Bai Dinh Pagoda – Discover Vietnam’s largest Buddhist complex.
• 19:00–21:00 → Dinner in Ninh Binh / return to Hanoi – End the day with a hearty meal.

Day 4: Beaches & Culture

• 07:00–09:00 → Flight Hanoi → Da Nang – Fly to Vietnam’s coastal gem.
• 09:30–11:00 → Hotel check-in & rest – Refresh before exploring.
• 11:30–15:00 → Relax at My Khe Beach – Swim or sunbathe on one of Asia’s best beaches.
• 15:30–17:30 → Visit Marble Mountains – Explore caves, pagodas, and panoramic views.
• 18:00–19:00 → Evening visit to Dragon Bridge – Watch the fire and water show.
• 19:30–21:00 → Dinner by the beach – Enjoy seafood with ocean breezes.

Day 5: Iconic Landmark

• 08:00–09:00 → Transfer to Ba Na Hills – Scenic Mountain Drive.
• 09:30–12:00 → Explore the famous Golden Bridge & cable car ride – Walk across the iconic “hands bridge.”
• 12:00–13:00 → Lunch at Ba Na Hills (French village area) – Dine in a European-style setting.
• 13:30–16:00 → Return & transfer to Hoi An Ancient Town – Journey to the lantern-lit heritage town.
• 16:30–18:00 → Walking tour of streets & Japanese Covered Bridge – Discover Hoi An’s timeless charm.
• 19:00–21:00 → Dinner in Hoi An / return to Da Nang – Savor central Vietnamese cuisine.

Day 6: Nightlife & Local Life

• 09:00–12:00 → Optional sightseeing at Son Tra Peninsula – Visit the Lady Buddha statue and nature reserve.
• 12:00–14:00 → Lunch – Try Da Nang’s signature dishes like Mi Quang.
• 14:00–18:00 → Free time (spa, shopping, or beach) – Relax or explore at your own pace.
• 19:00–21:00 → Explore Han Market & street food crawl – Shop and taste local delicacies.
• 21:30–23:00 → Bars & live music – Experience Da Nang’s vibrant nightlife.

Day 7: Departure (Hanoi – Noi Bai Airport)

• 10:00 → Hotel check-out – Prepare for departure.
• 10:30–12:30 → Transfer to Noi Bai Airport – Head back to Hanoi for your flight.
• 14:00 → Check-in at Noi Bai Airport for departure → Flight departure as scheduled – End of your Vietnam adventure.
